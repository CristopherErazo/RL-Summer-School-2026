## Summary

Some problems are complex, but not real world. 

Now reinforcement learning is becoming a method/tool that is used inside of other contexts. 

What is missing to have something that is used in real life?

* RL is trial and error: in real world trial and error is not possible because wrong actions can be the last actions. Making decisions in real world is risky.

When we want to deploy systems in the real world there are a lot of thing that are not considered in the canonical env-agent interaction view.

**RL Challenges**
What are the parts of the problem that are affected when I go to real world.

* 1) Transition Model.
* 2) Reward Function. 
* 3) Policy. 


**Conclusion**

Benchmarks are nice, but is not the real goal. If we want to move from the result to the application we nee to take into consideration the full pipeline of deployment which is much complex than the beanchmark.
